01 · About

I design for the eight-AM print, nine-AM markup, ten-AM boardroom rhythm.

Most decks fall apart somewhere between the analyst's first draft and the partner's final printout. My job is to keep the argument intact through every revision — so the deck that lands on the boardroom table is structurally honest, on-brand, and impossible to misread.

I work in PowerPoint, Keynote, and Google Slides; I handle data viz in the Adobe Creative Suite when the deck engine can't do it justice. I run template libraries, mentor junior designers, and translate stakeholder edits into structure rather than chaos.

04 · Process

From a six-line brief to a printed handout in less than a working week.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Fast intake call. I leave with a single-sentence argument and a list of must-include exhibits.

  2. 02

    Wireframe

    Pencil thumbnails of every slide before any pixel is pushed. Cheap to throw away.

  3. 03

    Visual system

    Palette, type pair, and one motif locked. Charts use the same axis treatment across the deck.

  4. 04

    Build

    PowerPoint or Keynote master, then content. Adobe Suite for any chart the deck engine can't do justice.

  5. 05

    Critique

    Two reviewers — one for the argument, one for type and spacing. Edits land in 24 hours.

  6. 06

    Ship

    Screen, print, and PDF versions. Source files handed off with the asset library.
